# Version 0.6.4 plan: sharing pages and folders with other people

Written 2026-11-25. Asked for by the owner on 2026-11-25; the overview and his decisions are in `docs/v0.6-plan.md` ("Sharing with other people"). This file is the build plan, in slices. Each slice is committed on `feature/v0.6.4-sharing` with its tests; the pull request is opened when the version is complete.

## Goal

Rowan right-clicks a page or folder, chooses **Share…**, picks "Mila" from the people nearby (for example on his phone's hotspot), and Mila's tablet asks "Rowan wants to share Biology with you". She accepts; it appears in `Shared with me/Rowan/Biology` on all her devices. Both can edit it, also offline; when their devices meet, the changes are joined word by word. While both have the page open on the same network, it syncs every few seconds.

## What never changes

- No server and no account on any website. An "account" is a person: a name and the devices paired with each other.
- Only what is shared ever goes to another person. Their devices get no other access to the notes folder.
- Nothing arrives unasked: every share is accepted first.
- Stopping or leaving a share never deletes anyone's copy.
- Everything received from another person's device is untrusted: size limits, safe paths, nothing run.

## Slices

| Slice | What | State |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Joining edits word by word** (`textmerge.rs`, `sync/basetext.rs`). A page changed on two sides is joined against the text both had last; only when both changed the same words are both versions kept. Also for sync between one's own devices. | Built 2026-11-25 |
| 2 | **Your name and people nearby.** A name per person (Settings, Sync: "Your name"), with a random account id, given to every paired device (hello exchange; a device without one takes its partner's). A switch "Let people nearby see my name to share with me", off by default (opt-in rule). When on, the discovery note also carries the account id and name; a new note format that devices before 0.6.4 ignore. `Service::people_nearby()` lists the people heard who are not you. | Built 2026-11-25 |
| 3 | **Share records.** `.scalenote/shares.json` in the notes folder: per share an id, the owner and members (account id and name), the device keys known per account, the path here (owner: where it is; member: `Shared with me/<owner>/<name>`), and whether it is offered, active or left. Synced between one's own devices like the folder colours, merged per share; only listed for devices that say `shares=1` in their greeting, since older apps refuse unknown files in a list. | Built 2026-11-25 |
| 4 | **Invitations.** A connection from a device that is not paired is accepted for one request only: an invitation (size and rate limited). It is kept and shown ("Rowan wants to share Biology with you", Accept / No). Accept writes the share record and answers the owner's device, which marks it active. | Built 2026-11-25 |
| 5 | **Syncing a share.** The sync engine gets a path map: the shared item's own names ("share space") on both sides, mapped to `Biology/…` here and `Shared with me/Rowan/Biology/…` there, plus the pictures and PDFs its pages link to (kept at `attachments/…` on both sides). A share connection names the share first; the answering device checks that the caller's key belongs to that share, then serves only that share's files. Any device of either person that holds the share can sync it with any device of the other. Joining as in slice 1. | Built 2026-11-25 |
| 6 | **More often.** While a shared page is open and the other person's device can be reached, the share is synced a few seconds after each pause in typing and every 5 seconds; otherwise with the normal 30-second checks. | Next |
| 7 | **Screens.** Share… in the menu of pages and folders (people nearby, who it is shared with, Stop sharing); the invitation box; a shared mark in the page list; Leave for a member; the "Shared with me" folder. Language file, light and dark screenshots. | |
| 8 | **Wrap-up.** Rust tests with three services (Rowan with two devices, Mila with one): invite, accept, edits both ways, joining, a clash, stop and leave, a stranger's device refused. Security checklist, release notes, PROGRESS, README, version 0.6.4. | |

## Open questions (to settle while building)

- **New devices joining later.** A share lists the device keys of both people. When Mila pairs a new phone, it gets the share from her other devices (slice 3), but Rowan's devices do not know its key yet. Proposal: a device of a person in a share may introduce its own paired devices to the other person's devices when they next sync the share.
- **Rights.** Proposal for 0.6.4: everyone in a share can edit; only the owner can stop sharing or add people. Read-only sharing can come later.
