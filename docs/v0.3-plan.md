## 0.3.0 rules
- Delete is reversible: pages and folders go to a hidden `.trash` folder in the notes folder. Only Delete forever and Empty trash remove files, after a question.
- Search has no index; it reads the pages each time, with limits. An index may only ever be a cache that can be rebuilt from the files.
- The search box and the Trash box are the only new screens. The commands in the box are only ones that exist.
- Not in 0.3.0: jumping to the match inside the page, search in picture names, automatic emptying of the trash.

## Covers are optional
- A page has no cover unless the owner adds one, and a cover can be removed again. Pages without a cover look exactly as they do now.
- The owner confirmed both options (2026-11-06): a "Show covers" toggle, and per page either his own image or no image at all. A page's cover is an image the person picks from a file; there are no built-in stock images unless asked for later.
- A setting turns the whole feature off ("Show covers"), so when it is off no cover element or "Add cover" entry exists anywhere in the interface, in the same way as the AI off-switch. Whether it is on or off by default is decided when the slice is built.
- The cover is stored in a way that Obsidian ignores harmlessly (the exact form is chosen then, and written down in DECISIONS.md).

## Tags and favorites (0.3.6)
- A tag is `#name` in the text of a page, or an entry under `tags:` in the page's properties. Names follow Obsidian: letters, digits, `_`, `-` and `/`, at least one character that is not a digit (`#1984` is a number, `#y1984` is a tag), at most 100 letters, and the `#` must start a line or follow a space. A `/` makes a nested tag: `#school/maths` is listed under `#school` too. Tags are matched without regard to case.
- Not tags: text in code (spans and fences), the `#` of a link's heading (`[[Page#Heading]]`), `\#name`, and `word#name`.
- A tag stays ordinary text in the page. The editor only marks it, and clicking it opens the Tags box on that tag.
- The Tags box lists every tag with its number of pages, and the pages of the tag you pick. It is opened from the page list, from the search box ("Open tags") or by clicking a tag. The list is read from the files each time the box opens; nothing is stored.
- A favourite page has `favorite: true` in its properties. The star next to the title sets or removes it; the Favorites list above the page list shows the favourite pages and is not drawn while there are none. Obsidian shows the property as a checkbox.
- Not in 0.3.6: tag suggestions while typing `#`, renaming or merging tags, editing the `tags:` property from the interface, marking a page other than the open one as a favourite, colours for tags, a tag page of its own.
