This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Rename** in the right-click menu of a page or folder (press and hold on a touch screen, F2 with the keyboard).
- Renaming a folder keeps everything inside it, and an open page follows its new name.

## Changed
- Double-clicking a folder still renames it, and the field keeps focus when clicked into.
