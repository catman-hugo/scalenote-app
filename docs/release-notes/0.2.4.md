# ScaleNote 0.2.4

A small release. This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Right-click a folder or a page in the page list to get a menu with **Rename**. On a touch screen, press and hold instead. With the keyboard, press F2 on the entry, or the menu key.
- The name turns into a text field in the list. Enter keeps the new name, Escape cancels. A name that already exists, or an empty one, changes nothing.
- Renaming a folder keeps everything inside it, and a page you have open follows its new name.

## Changed
- Double-clicking a folder still renames it, but the field no longer loses focus when you click inside it.

## Not in this release
Delete, duplicate and share are not in the menu yet. Showing a preview of a block while you drag it, selecting several blocks and the plus button are planned for 0.2.5.
