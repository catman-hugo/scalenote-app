## New
- **Text styles.** Highlight, underline, subscript, superscript and text colour, from the selection bar or with shortcuts.
- **Eight text colours** that stay readable on every theme.
- Styles are saved in the page in a form other programs understand.

## Fixed
- On Android the formatting bar appears when text is selected with a finger.
