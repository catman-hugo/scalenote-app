This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Tags.** `#name` in a page becomes a tag, including nested tags like `#school/maths` and tags in the page properties.
- **Tags box.** Every tag with its page count, a filter, and the pages of the chosen tag.
- **Favorites.** A star next to the page title. Favorite pages are listed above the page list.

## Changed
- Pages with tags are edited like any other text and written back exactly as they were.
- The bottom row of the page list wraps to a second line when narrow.
