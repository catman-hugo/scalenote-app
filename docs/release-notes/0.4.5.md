# ScaleNote 0.4.5

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Paper for drawings. In the drawing toolbar there is a Paper menu: no lines, four sizes of rule lines (with a red margin line), or four sizes of grid. New drawings have no lines.
- Page colour for drawings. A second menu gives sixteen page colours, or No colour. The colours are adjusted to the theme, so they stay soft on a dark theme. Both choices can be undone and redone, and are saved with the drawing.
- Folder icons. Right-click a folder, or hold it on a touch screen, and choose Icon. Pick a style (the folder, a notebook or a line) and one of sixteen colours, or no colour. The choice is kept in a small hidden file inside your vault, so it moves with the vault. Your notes are not touched.

## Changed
- Shapes and sticky notes snap to the grid when you move them with the loop tool, the same grid as when you draw them. Pen lines in the selection move by the same amount. The setting "Snap shapes to a grid" turns it off.

## Good to know
- A drawing that uses paper lines or a page colour opens read-only in ScaleNote 0.4.4 and older. Nothing is lost, but use 0.4.5 on every device.
- A folder you rename in ScaleNote keeps its icon. A folder renamed in another program loses its icon.

## Known limits
- None of this has been tried yet on a real tablet with a pen and fingers.
- The other limits of 0.4.4 still apply.
