This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Paper.** No lines, four sizes of rule lines or four sizes of grid.
- **Page colour.** Sixteen colours that adjust to the theme.
- **Folder icons.** Right-click a folder to choose a style and one of sixteen colours.

## Changed
- Shapes and sticky notes snap to the grid when moved with the loop tool.
