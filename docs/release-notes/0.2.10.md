# ScaleNote 0.2.10

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Text and background colours now use the same ten-colour palette as Notion: Default, Gray, Brown, Orange, Yellow, Green, Blue, Purple, Pink and Red. Select some text and press the colour button (the letter A): one row sets the text colour, the other the background colour. You can use both on the same words. Default takes a colour off again.
- The colours follow your theme: a pale background in a light theme becomes a dark one in a dark theme, the way Notion does it.
- Settings has a new button, "Make a copy for Obsidian". It makes a copy of your pages in a new folder next to your notes folder. In the copy, background colours become a plain highlight and text colours are removed, so Obsidian shows no odd markup. Your own pages are not changed, and a copy is never replaced: making it again creates another one. Pictures and other files are copied too. Not available on Android for now.

## Changed
- The 0.2.9 text colours are still shown and kept. They are saved in the new form the next time you edit that paragraph.
- The plain highlight button (H) is unchanged. It is the one Obsidian also understands.

## Notes
- Colours are saved in your pages by name, as a small html tag. Other programs show the words but not the colour. That is what the Obsidian copy is for.
- A few text colours are slightly darker than Notion's own so that they stay readable on every ScaleNote theme.
- Underline, subscript and superscript are left as they are in the Obsidian copy.
