This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## Since 0.5.9

### New
- **Groundwork for choosing what each device keeps offline.** Sync can now leave a folder or page off a device on purpose without taking it for a deleted page, and remembers what your other devices have. The settings to choose this come in a later version; until then everything works as before and every device keeps everything.
- A folder or page whose files are not all on this device can only be renamed, moved or deleted on a device that has all of them, so nothing is left behind under the old name.

## Since 0.5.0

Version 0.5 brought sync between your own devices: no account and no central server, only your devices talking to each other directly.

### Sync
- **Pair your devices** with an 8-digit code, and find them on your network without typing an address.
- **Sync by itself** within about 15 seconds of a change, between computers, phones and tablets on the same network. The first sync with a device shows what it would do and asks first.
- **Nothing is lost**: a page changed on two devices keeps both versions, and a deleted page goes to the trash on the other devices.
- **Sync over Tailscale** for eduroam and other networks that keep devices apart.
- **Each paired device syncs one notes folder**, so folders never mix.
- **Wi-Fi only** per device, **Pause sync**, a status line, and a list of what each sync did.
- **Home server (Linux)**, an optional always-on device to sync with, and an add-only inbox for it.
- Groundwork for choosing what each device keeps offline (see above).

### Privacy and safety
- **App lock** with a PIN or passcode.
- Android: **fingerprint or face unlock**.
- **Backups** of your notes folder.
- A **Privacy tab** and security cards that say plainly what ScaleNote does on the network.

### Pages, drawings and PDFs
- **Find words in PDF pages and picture captions**, and resize a PDF in a page.
- **Pages open in the view you left them in**, a cover and "Linked from" per page.
- **Move and resize canvas regions** by their grip, and the canvas can start at the top left, like a page.
- A **colour per folder**.

### Settings and the app
- **Settings search**, a **This device** tab, an About page with the app's log, and themes as cards, with true white and true black.
- Computers: **updates install from inside the app**, zoom with Ctrl + and Ctrl −, a shortcuts page, tray and open at login.
- Android: zoom setting, back gesture and back button everywhere, much faster PDF import as pictures, better sizes on a phone.
- The app starts on the page you had open last.
- A **setup guide** in the docs folder.
