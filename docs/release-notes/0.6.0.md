This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Groundwork for choosing what each device keeps offline.** Sync can now leave a folder or page off a device on purpose without taking it for a deleted page, and remembers what your other devices have. The settings to choose this come in the next version; until then everything works as before and every device keeps everything.
- A folder or page whose files are not all on this device can only be renamed, moved or deleted on a device that has all of them, so nothing is left behind under the old name.
