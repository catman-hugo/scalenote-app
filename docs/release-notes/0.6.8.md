# ScaleNote 0.6.8

The last version of the 0.6 line: fixes from testing with three devices and a tablet pen, and a check of the whole line.

## New
- **Tablet pen buttons can switch to the eraser.** Some tablet pens send a key when a button is tapped with the pen away from the screen. Under Settings, Drawing, give each of those keys a job while you draw: switch to the eraser and back, undo, or redo. Tap once to switch, again to switch back. The Pen test (at the bottom of the page list) shows which key each button sends.

## Fixed
- **Sync now no longer says "A sync is already running" nearly every time.** With three or more devices one is often syncing; Sync now and Compare now wait for it (up to a minute), and a sync from another device waits a few seconds too.
- **Dark pen colours stay readable in a dark theme.** Charcoal, navy and indigo vanished on a dark page. They are now drawn a little lighter there, only as far as needed; on a light page nothing changes.
- **"Available offline" is now called "Keep on this device"**, which is what it does: all your notes are files on your devices, and this chooses what this device keeps a copy of. Its two choices now sit next to their words.

## Good to know
- Two libraries inside the app's window toolkit had security fixes this week; this version has them.
- The setup guide now explains three or more devices and what each device keeps.
