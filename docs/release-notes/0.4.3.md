# ScaleNote 0.4.3

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## Changed
- The line width of the pen is now a slider with five stops, like in OneNote. Open the width button in the drawing toolbar and slide; the line under the slider and the button show the width. The arrow keys also move it one stop. Thin, medium and thick from before are three of the five stops, and the new ones are in between and above.
- The page id is no longer shown while you use the app. Following a link to a page no longer shows it at the top, the plain markdown view leaves it out, the "Text" view of the page history leaves it out, and a search never matches or shows it. It is still in the file, so history keeps working, and it is put back exactly when you save from the plain markdown view.

- Shapes snap to a grid. Rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows now put their corners on an invisible grid, so shapes drawn at different times line up exactly instead of missing each other by a pixel. It is on by default; turn it off in Settings, under Drawing, with "Snap shapes to a grid". Shapes already drawn are not moved.
- The pen colours are the same sixteen as in OneNote: three rows of six, six and four (yellow to light green, and black, two greys and light grey). Black is still the one that follows the theme. The colours are the same in light and dark, so dark grey is hard to see on the dark theme. Older drawings open as before; their red, blue, green, orange and purple are now the brighter versions.
- The bar that shows the line width has rounded ends.

## Fixed
- A page whose file starts with an invisible byte-order mark (some Windows editors add one) showed its properties, with the id, as a block of text at the top. It is now read as normal properties.
- A page with an id but no text (for example one made by following a link to a page that does not exist yet) showed an empty "Kept exactly as written" block. It now opens as an empty page.
- A page that is shown as one block of kept-as-written text no longer includes its properties in that block.

- Zooming with two fingers on the drawing sheet no longer makes the drawing drift away. The spot between your fingers now stays under them while you zoom in or out, and moving both fingers together still moves the sheet.

## Good to know
- Your drawings and pages are unchanged. A drawing with a line width that is not one of the five stops is kept as it is and the slider shows the nearest stop.
- The id is only visible when you open the file in another program.

## Known limits
- The slider has been tried with a mouse and with test events, not yet with a real pen and fingers on a screen.
- The other limits of 0.4.2 still apply.
