This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

**Update every device to 0.7.0 before you make an animation.** An older version opens a page with an animation but cannot change its drawing (it shows it read-only, so nothing is lost). The sync fix of 0.6.9 also needs both devices to have it.

## Since 0.6.9

### New: animations in pages
- **Type /animation** in a page. A new animation appears in the text, and the canvas opens beside it with a bar of steps under the drawing.
- **Draw it step by step.** Duplicate step copies the step you are on, so you only erase or add what changes. New step, Move left and right, and Delete step are on the same bar. Every change can be undone. The `,` and `.` keys change the step without putting the pen down.
- **A background for all steps.** With "Background (all steps)" on, what you draw shows in every step: a membrane, the labels, a receptor. Fix a label once and it is fixed everywhere.
- **Before and after.** Switch it on to see the step before faintly in blue and the step after in orange while you draw. They are only a guide and are never saved or printed.
- **A caption for each step**: a title and a line of text, shown under the animation.
- **Show it three ways.** Right-click the animation in the text (or long-press on a phone or tablet):
  - **Steps**: Back, Next and a dot for each step. You can also swipe sideways on a phone or tablet, or use the arrow keys.
  - **Video**: press Play; it plays once and stops on the last step.
  - **Loop (GIF)**: it plays by itself, over and over, and pauses when it is scrolled out of sight. If your system asks for less motion, it waits for Play.
  - Choose the speed for a video or a loop: 1 to 24 steps a second, or a speed of your own from 0.5 to 30.
- **On paper**, printing or saving as PDF shows every step one after another with its caption, which makes a study sheet.
- **On a phone or tablet** the step bar stays at the bottom of the screen in one line. Swipe on it to change the step, or tap "2 / 7" to see all steps as small pictures: tap one to go there, or drag it to reorder.
- Double-click an animation in the text to draw on it again. On the canvas, move or resize its box like a canvas region.

### Good to know
- An animation is kept in the page's own drawing file, so it syncs, goes to the trash and comes back with its page. Obsidian shows the block in the text as a small code block with the animation's id.
- While you draw an animation, everything you draw goes into its step, also outside the box. Press Done to draw on the page again.

## Since 0.6.0

Version 0.6 added choosing what each device keeps, sharing with someone nearby, and getting things into ScaleNote from other apps; 0.7.0 adds animations.

### Animations (0.7.0)
- Draw something in steps and show it in a page as steps, a video or a loop (see above).

### Sync and devices
- **Keep on this device** per folder or page; pages that are not here open on demand. Settings, Storage shows how much room each folder takes and can free up space.
- **Sync over a phone's hotspot or a USB cable**, without internet.
- **Folders sync**: empty, moved and deleted folders too. Folder colours are kept.
- **Pen writing over the text** shows on your other devices right after a sync.
- Edits made on two devices are **joined word by word**.
- Sync now waits for a sync that is already running. Both devices remember what they had after a sync, so a renamed page no longer comes back under its old name and editing no longer makes needless conflict copies.
- A **conflicts button** next to the sync status opens the conflict copies.
- Search finds pages on your other devices.

### Sharing and getting things in
- **Share a page or folder with someone nearby.** They accept first, and it arrives in Shared with me.
- **Share text, links, pictures and PDFs into ScaleNote** from other apps. On computers there is also Send to and Open with, and a new page from the clipboard, the tray or Ctrl+Alt+N.

### Pages, drawings and PDFs
- PDFs of any length as pictures (up to 64 MB). "Apply to all slides" gives every page of a PDF the same size.
- Writing on a PDF slide stays on the slide on every screen size.
- Find in page (Ctrl+F) cleans up after itself and no longer jumps when closed.
- A page opens at the height where you left it.
- Pages have icons like folders. Inbox and Shared with me are at the top of the page list.
- Tablet pen buttons that send a key can switch to the eraser, undo or redo. Dark pen colours stay readable in a dark theme.
