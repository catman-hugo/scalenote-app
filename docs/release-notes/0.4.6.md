This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Split view.** Text and drawing side by side (stacked on a phone), next to Text and Canvas.
- **Open pages in** setting: Text, Canvas or Split.

## Changed
- Highlights are always drawn behind pen lines and shapes.
- **Smart highlight.** A highlight that goes a little over a rectangle, ellipse or sticky note is cut at its edge.
