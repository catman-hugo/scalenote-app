# ScaleNote 0.4.6

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## Changed
- Highlights are always behind your pen lines and shapes, also when you highlighted last. A highlight can still be drawn on a sticky note, and the text of the note stays in front.
- Smart highlight. When a highlight covers a rectangle, an ellipse or a sticky note and only goes a little over its edge, it is cut off at the edge, so it looks clean. A highlight that goes clearly outside the figure is left as you drew it, so you can still highlight beside a figure. Your drawing is not changed by this: only how it is shown.

## Good to know
- You can no longer put a highlight on top of a pen line on purpose.
- Where several sticky notes overlap, the text of the lower note can show through the note above it.

## Known limits
- Not yet tried on a real tablet with a pen. How much overshoot counts as "a little" may need adjusting after you try it.
- The other limits of 0.4.5 still apply.
