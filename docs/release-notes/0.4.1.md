# ScaleNote 0.4.1

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- More tools on the Canvas of a page:
  - Highlighter: a wide, see-through line. It uses the colour and width you chose.
  - Shapes: line, arrow, rectangle and ellipse. Drag to draw one.
  - Sticky note: tap on the sheet, type, and press Ctrl+Enter or tap elsewhere to finish. Tap a note again to change its text. A note with no text is not kept.
  - Select with a loop: draw a loop around what you want. Drag the selection to move it. The trash button, the Delete key or Escape (to let go) work on it.
- The eraser removes a shape by touching its outline, and a note by touching it.
- Undo and Redo also take back moves, deletes and changes to a note.
- Settings has a new "Drawing" part with "Pen button": what the button on the side of a pen does. It erases by default, or it can select with a loop, or do nothing. The eraser end of a pen always erases.

## Good to know
- A drawing that only has pen lines is saved just like in 0.4.0, so an older version can still open it. As soon as it has a highlighter line, a shape or a note, an older version will show it as "from a newer version" and will not change it.

## Known limits
- You cannot resize a note or a shape, change the colour of something already drawn, copy and paste, or write text outside a note.
- You cannot draw over the text of a page yet, and there is no split view or PDF import. These come in the next slices of 0.4.
- A loop selects a whole stroke, never part of one.
- A drawing is not in the page history, is not exported with "Export this page", and is not printed.
- The new tools have been tried with a mouse and with test events, not yet on a real pen and screen. Typing a note with the Android on-screen keyboard has not been tried.
