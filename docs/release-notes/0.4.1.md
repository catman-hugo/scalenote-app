This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Highlighter**, a wide see-through line.
- **Shapes:** line, arrow, rectangle and ellipse.
- **Sticky notes** with text.
- **Select with a loop** to move or delete drawings.
- **Pen button setting** in Settings under Drawing.
- Undo and Redo cover moves, deletes and note changes.
