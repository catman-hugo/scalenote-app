# ScaleNote 0.4.2

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## Changed
- The drawing toolbar is smaller. Shapes, colour and line width are now menus: press the button to open it, choose, and it closes. Each button shows what is chosen now. "Finger draws" and "Show all" are in the new "more" menu (the three dots). The toolbar is one line on a wide window and two on a phone, so the drawing sheet is taller.
- Pressing the shape button also switches to the shape you used last, so one press starts drawing and also shows the other shapes.
- Settings, Tags and Trash at the bottom of the page list are now buttons with an icon and a label, like New page and New folder. When the list is narrow, only the icons show.

## Good to know
- Nothing about your drawings or pages changed.
- To give the sheet the full width on a tablet, hide the page list with the menu button at the top left.

## Known limits
- The menus have been tried with a mouse and with test events, not yet with a real pen and fingers on a screen.
- The other limits of 0.4.1 still apply.
