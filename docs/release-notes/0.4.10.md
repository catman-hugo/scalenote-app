This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Draw on the text.** A pen button on the top bar opens a pen bar and a see-through sheet over the page. The ink stays on the paragraph, heading or list it was drawn on.
- **Columns.** `/2 columns` or `/3 columns`, or drag a block to the side of another block. Up to five columns, saved as Obsidian-style callouts.
- **Update inside the Android app.** Download in the update notice installs the new version through Android's installer.
