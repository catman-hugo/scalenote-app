# ScaleNote 0.4.10

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## Changed
- Fixed: a bulleted list, a numbered list and a to-do list written one after another opened as one grey "Kept exactly as written" block of plain text. They now open as three lists you can edit. Your text was never changed by this, only shown the wrong way.
- Two lists of different kinds (a different bullet, or numbered after bulleted) with a blank line between them are now always read as two lists.

## Good to know
- A list with blank lines between its own items (a "loose" list) is still shown as "Kept exactly as written". That is on purpose: the editor would otherwise remove the blank lines the first time you change it. Making these editable is possible later.

## Known limits
- Two-finger zoom on Linux still zooms the whole window, and finger drawing on the canvas can still move the screen instead of drawing. Known, not fixed in this version.
- Drawing over text, columns and the Android in-app installer are planned for 0.4.11.
