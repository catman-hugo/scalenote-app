This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Sync over Tailscale**, for networks such as eduroam where devices cannot see each other. With Tailscale on, Settings, Sync shows this device's Tailscale address, and the pairing code shows it too, so you can pair from anywhere. Devices that synced once tell each other their Tailscale address, and when a device cannot be found on the network, ScaleNote reaches it there by itself.
- **Each page remembers how you left it** on this device: in text, split or canvas view. "Open pages in" (Settings, Drawing) is used for pages you have not opened yet.
- **Cover and "Linked from" per page.** Add, change or remove a page's cover, and show or hide its "Linked from" list, from the page's View menu or by right-clicking the page in the list. The settings for all pages are gone.
- **A new Privacy tab**, last in Settings, holds what sync does with your pages and everything ScaleNote contacts.
- **Move and resize a canvas region by hand.** Each region (what a /canvas block in the text shows) has a grip next to its top left corner, like the handle next to a block in the text. Drag it to move the region with any tool in hand; after that, drag a corner to make the region bigger or smaller.
- **The /canvas block follows the pen.** In the split view, the block in the text shows a line while you are still drawing it, instead of after the drawing is saved.

## Fixed
- Android: **Adding a PDF as pictures is much faster on a phone.** Turning each page into a picture took about 5 seconds a page on a phone and a fortieth of a second on a computer. It now makes each picture straight away.
- **The Sync tab is shorter.** Your paired devices are at the top as clear cards, then the buttons to pair. The fields to enter a code only show when you choose "Enter a code".
- **Tick boxes in Settings** are bigger and sit right in front of their words.
- Drawing settings no longer say "Drawing" twice.
- Computers: **Ctrl P prints the page**, as the Shortcuts list said. It did nothing before.
- **Copy the app's log** says "Copied!" on the button. Before, nothing showed that it worked.
