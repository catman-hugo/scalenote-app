# ScaleNote 0.4.0

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Canvas. Every page now has a "Canvas" button at the top, next to "Markdown". It switches the page to a drawing sheet; "Text" takes you back. Opening another page also goes back to the text. The text of the page is never changed by what you draw.
- Pen with pressure: press lightly for a thin line and firmly for a thick one. A mouse draws a plain line. Six colours (black or white following the theme, red, blue, green, orange, purple) and three widths.
- Eraser: removes the whole stroke it touches. Undo and Redo (buttons, Ctrl+Z, Ctrl+Shift+Z) take back or bring back a stroke or an erase.
- Moving around: a finger moves the sheet and two fingers zoom, so a resting hand never draws. If your device has no pen, turn on "Finger draws". With a mouse, use the "Move the sheet" tool, the wheel, or hold space; Ctrl and the wheel zoom. "Show all" brings the whole drawing into view.
- A pen's side button (or its eraser end) erases while you hold it.
- The drawing is saved a moment after you stop, in a file called `Name.canvas.json` next to the page (`Name.md`). Obsidian ignores that file. It follows the page when the page is renamed, moved, deleted to the trash or restored.

## Known limits
- You cannot draw over the text of a page yet, and there is no highlighter, shapes, sticky notes, selecting or moving strokes, or PDF import. These come in the next slices of 0.4.
- The eraser removes a whole stroke, not part of it.
- A drawing is limited to 20,000 strokes. When it is full you get a message.
- A drawing is not in the page history, is not exported with "Export this page", and is not printed.
- If you rename or move a page with another program, its drawing file stays behind under the old name.
- If another program changes the drawing file while the canvas is open, ScaleNote stops saving that drawing and says so, and what you drew since the last save is not saved.
- The pen has been tried only with a mouse and with test events, not yet on a real pen and a real screen.
