This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Canvas.** Every page has a drawing sheet, opened with the Canvas button. The text is never changed by drawing.
- **Pressure pen** with six colours and three widths.
- **Eraser** that removes a whole stroke, with Undo and Redo.
- **Moving around.** A finger moves the sheet, two fingers zoom, and "Finger draws" is a switch for devices without a pen.
- **Pen side button** erases while held.
- The drawing is saved next to the page as `Name.canvas.json` and follows renames, moves and the trash.
