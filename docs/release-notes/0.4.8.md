This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Links follow renames.** Renaming a page updates the links to it in other pages.
- **Foldable tags.** Main tags such as #school fold their subtags.
- **History as the page looks.** Older versions show headings, lists, bold, tables and toggles.
- **View menu.** Text, Canvas and Split in one menu, so the top bar fits one row on a phone.

## Changed
- Toggles keep their outline whether folded or open, and open folded the first time a page is opened after starting the app.
- Empty lines are kept.

## Fixed
- On Linux, text no longer turns bold in long pages.
