This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **App lock** (Settings, This device; off by default). A PIN or passcode is asked when ScaleNote starts and again after the time away you choose. After 5 wrong tries each try waits longer. It keeps people out of ScaleNote; it does not encrypt your pages, and a forgotten PIN cannot be recovered.
- Android: **Fingerprint or face** can unlock the app lock.
- **Each paired device belongs to one notes folder**, the one open when you paired. It only syncs while that folder is open, so two folders or two server vaults never mix. Settings, Sync shows the folder and can move a pairing.
- **Find in PDF pages added as pictures**, and in the descriptions under pictures. PDFs added as pictures before this version need to be added again to be searchable.
- **A PDF in a page can be made narrower** with the squares on its sides; the width is saved.
- Computers: **Zoom:** Ctrl + and Ctrl − (80% to 200%), Ctrl 0 to go back. Also under Settings, Look.
- Android: **Zoom** under Settings, Look (80% to 200%).
- Computers: **Shortcuts page** (Settings, Shortcuts, or Ctrl /), printable on one sheet.
- **About page** (Settings, About): version, build day, What's new and a link to all versions.
- Computers: **Updates from inside the app.** From the next version on, Download in the update notice gets the new version in the same kind of file you installed (.msi, .exe, .deb, .rpm or AppImage), checks that it comes from ScaleNote's own builds, installs it and restarts ScaleNote. A .deb or .rpm asks for your password. The portable Windows zip still downloads in the browser.
- **Setup guide** from installing to syncing, a home server, the app lock and backups (`docs/setup-guide.md`).

## Fixed
- Android: Text and spacing were too big on a phone; they now follow the screen size. Settings no longer sits under the status bar.
- Android: The back gesture inside Settings or another window closed the app. It now goes from a Settings group back to the list, and otherwise closes the window.
- Linux: Two fingers on a drawing zoomed the whole window, and a finger scrolled the page instead of drawing or moving the sheet.
- Find in a PDF in a page now marks only the letters typed, not the whole line.
- Pairing and syncing by hand are closed while the app is locked.
- What's new in the app now only lists what changed on your system. It is a short list, with a button to these full notes, unless the update was downloaded with the app's own Download button on a computer.
- Android: The title of What's new no longer sits under the status bar.
