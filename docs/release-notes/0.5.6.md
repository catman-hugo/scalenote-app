This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about. 0.5.6 is being built; these notes grow as it does.

## Fixed (from your testing of 0.5.5)
- **Find (Ctrl+F) finds words in PDF pages added as pictures.** When a PDF is added as pictures, the words of each page are now kept in a small file next to its picture, and Find marks the words on the picture. PDFs added as pictures before this version are not searchable; add them again to make them searchable.
- **Find also searches the description under a picture** (for example "Page 2 of Lecture 1").
- **A PDF in a page can be made narrower.** Drag the small square on its left or right side (or select it and use the arrow keys). The width is saved with the page; dragging it back to full width removes it again.
- **Find in a PDF in a page marks only the letters you typed**, not the whole line they are in.
- **Smaller on a phone.** The app measures how wide the screen is. On a phone, text and spacing are a little smaller (about seven eighths, a bit less on very narrow screens), so more fits. The Text size setting still works on top of this. Computers and tablets are unchanged.
- **Settings on a phone no longer sits under the clock and status bar.**
- **Drawing with fingers on Linux.** Two fingers on the drawing zoomed the whole window instead of the drawing, and a finger that should draw (or pan the sheet) scrolled the page. The drawing now keeps every touch to itself, on the canvas and while drawing over text. Not yet tried on a Linux touch screen.

## New
- **App lock** (Settings, This device; off by default). A PIN or passcode is asked when ScaleNote starts and again after the time away you choose (from "leaving the app" to an hour). On an Android phone you can also unlock with a fingerprint or face. After 5 wrong tries each try has to wait longer. The lock keeps people out of ScaleNote; it does not encrypt your pages. If you forget it, ScaleNote cannot be opened on that device.
- **Zoom:** Ctrl + and Ctrl − make everything bigger or smaller (80% to 200%), Ctrl 0 goes back to 100%. Also under Settings, Look.
- **Shortcuts page** (Settings, Shortcuts, or Ctrl /) with every keyboard shortcut, and a button to print them on one sheet.
- **About page** (Settings, About): the version, the day it was built, its What's new, a link to all versions, and how ScaleNote is made.
