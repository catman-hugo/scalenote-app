This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about. The Android parts have only been built, not tried on a phone or tablet.

## New
- **Android can find other devices.** While ScaleNote is open on the phone it now listens for the short "I am here" notes that other devices send, so "Find devices" and automatic sync work from the phone too. The listening stops when the app leaves the screen.
- **Android back gesture.** Going back now returns to the page you were on before, and only leaves the app from the first page.
- **Back and Forward buttons** in the top bar, for every page you opened since the app started.
- **Find in this page button** in the top bar, for phones and tablets without a keyboard.
- **True black theme** (Settings, Look). The page is pure black, which saves battery on an OLED screen.
- **Search settings.** A box at the top of Settings finds a setting by its words across all tabs.
- **This device tab** in Settings, with "Open the page I was on when the app starts" and **Reset to defaults** (asks twice). Reset puts the look, page, drawing and update settings back; your notes, the open folder and paired devices are not touched.
- **The app starts on the page you had open last** (also after Android closed it to free memory). It can be turned off in Settings, This device.
- **Sync only on Wi-Fi, per device** (Android only, in Settings, Sync). A device set this way is skipped while the phone is not on Wi-Fi.
- **Back from the background.** If the app was away for a minute or more, it says that Android may have paused syncing and catches up.

## Changed
- The Sync tab on Android explains that syncing happens while ScaleNote is open.
