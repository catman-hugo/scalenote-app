This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Search.** Ctrl+K or the Search button looks in titles and text of all pages and shows the best matches first.
- **Commands** in the same box: New page, New folder, Open settings, Open trash and more.
- **Delete** for pages and folders. Everything goes to the trash.
- **Trash.** A Trash button lists deleted items. Restore puts them back, and deleting forever asks first.

## Fixed
- Enter on a button in Settings no longer closes the box.
- A page name ending in an accented letter no longer breaks the page list.
