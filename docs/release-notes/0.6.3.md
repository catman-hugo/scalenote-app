This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Choose what each device keeps offline.** Right-click (or long-press) a folder or page and choose **Available offline…**: follow the folder above, or keep only some kinds here (text, drawings, pictures, PDFs and other files). Settings, Files has the same choice for the whole notes folder.
- **Pages that are not on this device stay in the list** with a cloud icon. Opening one brings it (with its drawing and pictures) from one of your other devices that can be reached.
- **Switching something off frees space** on this device, but only for files another of your devices already has. Anything changed or new stays until it has synced.
- A folder or page whose files are not all on this device can only be renamed, moved or deleted on a device that has all of them.
