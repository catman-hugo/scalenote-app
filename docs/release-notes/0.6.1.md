This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Sync without Wi-Fi or internet.** Make a hotspot on your phone (mobile data can be off) or on your laptop, or connect your phone to your computer with a USB cable and turn on USB tethering. Your devices find each other on it and sync as they do at home. This also works at school, where eduroam keeps devices apart. The setup guide has the steps.
- **"Wi-Fi only" allows a hotspot or a cable**, since those never use mobile data.
- **The pairing screen shows every address of this device**, for example its home address and its hotspot address, so you can type the right one when the other device does not find it.
