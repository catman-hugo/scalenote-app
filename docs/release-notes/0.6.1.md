This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Sync without Wi-Fi or internet.** Make a hotspot on your phone (mobile data can be off) or on your laptop, or connect your phone to your computer with a USB cable and turn on USB tethering. Your devices find each other on it and sync as they do at home. This also works on school networks that keep devices apart. The setup guide has the steps.
- **"Wi-Fi only" allows a hotspot or a cable**, since those never use mobile data.
- **The pairing screen shows every address of this device**, for example its home address and its hotspot address, so you can type the right one when the other device does not find it.
- **No more page limit for PDFs as pictures** (it was 100). For a long PDF (over 100 pages, or pictures of over 100 MB) ScaleNote first says about how much space the pictures will take, in your notes folder and on every device that syncs it, and asks before it starts.
- **PDFs up to 64 MB** instead of 25 MB, the most sync can send in one file. Above 25 MB the question how to add it also says how big the file is.

- **Two fingers move and zoom while Finger draws is on.** On a canvas two fingers move and zoom the sheet, in a page they scroll it; one finger still draws.
- **Settings, Sync shows this device's address on the network** (and its hotspot address), above the Tailscale line.

## Fixed
- **Folder colours are kept when syncing.** Colours set on one device were wiped when the other device had a newer settings file; folders are now synced one by one. Colours that were already lost have to be set once more.
- **Cancel closes what Compare shows.** It did nothing before.
