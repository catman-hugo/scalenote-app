# ScaleNote 0.3.5

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Covers: a page can have a picture above its text. Covers are optional and **off by default**. Turn them on in Settings ("Show covers on pages"). While the switch is off, no cover is shown and there is no "Add cover" button anywhere.
- With covers on, every page shows "Add cover" above its text. Choose a picture from your files (PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP, up to 10 MB). "Change cover" picks another one and "Remove cover" takes it off the page. There are no built-in pictures: a cover is always one you chose.
- The picture is copied into the attachments folder of your notes, like a picture inside a page. The page itself only gets one extra line in its properties at the top, `cover: attachments/name.png`. Obsidian shows that as an ordinary property and otherwise ignores it. Every other property is left exactly as it was.

## Changed
- Nothing changes for pages without a cover, or when covers are off.

## Known limits
- Removing a cover leaves the picture file in the attachments folder. Delete it from there yourself if you do not want it.
- The picture is always cropped to fill the same wide, short strip; you cannot move or resize it.
- If the properties at the top of a page have a `cover` entry written over several lines (a list, for example), ScaleNote does not change it and says so. Change it in the markdown text view instead.
