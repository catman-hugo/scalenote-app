This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **PDF pages are added in the background.** Adding a PDF as pictures now shows a small progress card with the pages done and the time left. You can keep writing or open other pages while it runs, and the pictures arrive in the page you started from. Stop is on the card, and only one PDF is added at a time.
- **Sync tab in Settings.** It has a switch for sync (on by default) and a name for this device. Your choices are saved on this device only, never inside your notes folder.
- **Groundwork for sync.** The app can now work out what two copies of your notes would need to become the same, without changing any file. Nothing is sent anywhere yet: pairing your devices comes in a later version.

## Changed
- Adding a PDF as pictures is a little faster. The pictures themselves are exactly as before.
