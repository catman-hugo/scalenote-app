# ScaleNote 0.3.2

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Touch screens: dragging a page or folder in the page list now works with a finger. Hold your finger still on an entry for a moment (you feel a short buzz and it is highlighted), then move it onto a folder, or onto the empty space below the list for the top level. Near the top or bottom edge the list scrolls by itself.
- Touch screens: there are now two ways to open the menu (Rename, Move to, Delete): hold your finger still for longer (about a second), or tap the same entry twice quickly. On a phone the first tap on a page opens it and closes the list, so there the long hold is the way.
- New pages and new folders go to the top level when no folder is open. They only go into a folder while that folder, and the folders around it, are open. Before, a folder you had touched earlier kept receiving new things even when it was folded shut. Drag the new entry where you want it.

## Changed
- Moving a finger straight away still scrolls the list, as before.
- The phone's own long-press menu no longer appears in the page list. It started at the same moment as the drag and got in the way.
- Dragging with a mouse or pen is smoother: the preview moves without laying out the page again, and the folder under the pointer is looked up once per screen refresh instead of for every tiny movement. A drag that the system interrupts no longer leaves the list stuck.
