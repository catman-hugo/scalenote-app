This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Touch dragging.** Hold a page or folder, then drag it onto a folder or the empty space. The list scrolls near its edges.
- **Menu on touch screens.** Hold longer, or tap the same entry twice.

## Changed
- New pages and folders go to the top level unless a folder is open.
- Dragging with a mouse or pen is smoother.
