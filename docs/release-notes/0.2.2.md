# ScaleNote 0.2.2

A polish release based on testing 0.2.1. This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## Changed
- Click the folder name at the top left of the page list to switch folders. The menu shows your recent folders, "Open another folder" and "Use the default folder". The separate "Open another folder" button is gone.
- Settings and "What's new" can now be closed with their close button, with Escape, or by clicking outside the box. The first-run screens still cannot be closed, because a choice is needed there.
- There are now five text sizes: Smallest, Small, Normal, Large and Largest. Icons grow and shrink with the text.
- "Note" is now "page" in the screens: "New page", "Pages", "Page width".
- Toggle blocks: opening and closing now works. A toggle shows only its arrow and title until you click inside it.

## Not in this release
Showing a preview of a block while you drag it, selecting several blocks, and the plus button are planned for 0.2.3.
