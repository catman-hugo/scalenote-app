This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Folder switcher.** The folder name at the top left opens a menu with recent folders, "Open another folder" and "Use the default folder".
- **Five text sizes.** Smallest to Largest. Icons scale with the text.

## Changed
- Settings and What's new close with their button, Escape or a click outside.
- "Note" is now "page" in the screens.

## Fixed
- Toggles open and close again. A toggle shows only its arrow and title until it is clicked into.
