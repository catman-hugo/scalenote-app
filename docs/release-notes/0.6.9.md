Fixes and requests after testing 0.6.8 on a laptop and a phone.

## Fixed
- **Sync no longer makes double pages or needless conflict copies.** Only the device that started a sync remembered what both devices had afterwards. So when the other device started the next sync, a page renamed in the meantime came back under its old name, and an edit became a conflict copy. Now both devices remember it. The same fault made a phone show "First sync" after weeks of syncing; that is gone too. Both devices need 0.6.9 for this.
- **Find in page (Ctrl+F) cleans up after itself.** Marks on PDF pages and their captions now go away when you clear the search or close the find bar. Marks no longer end up in the wrong place on a slide. Closing the find bar no longer jumps to where your cursor was; the page stays where it is, and the cursor goes to the word you found.
- **Writing on a PDF slide stays on the slide.** Writing on a page picture used to slide off the slide on a wider screen, and go off-screen on a narrower one. Now it moves and scales with the slide, on every screen size. Writing done before this version stays where it was.

## New
- **A page opens where you left it.** Going to another page and back puts the page at the same height, instead of at the cursor or the top.
- **"Apply to all slides".** After you resize one page of a PDF added as pictures, this button gives every page of that PDF the same size. One undo puts them all back.
- **Inbox and "Shared with me" are at the top of the page list**, above your other folders.
- **Pages have icons, like folders.** Right-click a page, then Icon: choose a page, notebook or line, and a colour.
- **A conflicts button next to the sync status.** It shows how many conflict copies your notes folder has and opens them. It goes away after the last one is dealt with.
- **A setting for writing on pictures in a dark theme** (Settings, Drawing; off by default). When it is on, writing on a slide keeps the colours it has on a light page, so black writing stays black on a white slide.

## Tidier
- Pages in the page list line up with the folders beside them, and an empty folder has no arrow.
- "Show “Linked from”" in a page's menu now starts where the other items start.
- Close is at the bottom right in Settings and in every other box. On a phone, Back sits next to it.
