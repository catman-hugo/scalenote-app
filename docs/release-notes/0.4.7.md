# ScaleNote 0.4.7

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Recolour a selection. Pick the loop tool, circle some drawings, then pick a colour from the colour menu: everything you circled changes to that colour at once. One Undo puts them all back. The colour of the next line you draw does not change.

## Changed
- Smart cutting now also works for the pen. A pen line that goes a little over a rectangle, an ellipse or a sticky note is cut off at the edge. A pen line that stays inside is left as it was.
- The cut is now part of the drawing. If you remove the rectangle later, the highlight (or pen line) stays cut and the part that went over does not come back.
- If one long stroke goes far outside the figure, only the part that went far outside is shown there; the rest stays cut. The stroke is stored as a few pieces, and one Undo removes all of them together.
- Moving a cut stroke moves its cut with it.

- Fixed: the search box could sometimes stay on "Searching" when it was opened again right after closing it.

## Good to know
- A drawing that contains a cut is saved in a newer drawing format. ScaleNote 0.4.6 and older open it read-only instead of changing it, so nothing is lost, but you cannot edit it there. Drawings without a cut are saved as before.
- Strokes drawn before 0.4.7 are not cut. Only new strokes are.

## Known limits
- Not yet tried on a real tablet with a pen. How far a stroke may go over before it counts as "far" may need adjusting.
- The other limits of 0.4.6 still apply.
