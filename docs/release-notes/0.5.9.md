This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Canvas edges** (Settings, Drawing). Choose "Start at the top left, like a page" and the canvas stops at its top and left edge, like a page in OneNote, while it stays open to the right and down as far as you want. The ruled paper's margin line then stays at the left instead of ending up in the middle of the screen. The default stays "Open on every side". Anything already drawn above or left of the edge stays reachable.
