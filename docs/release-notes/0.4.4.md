# ScaleNote 0.4.4

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## Changed
- Sticky notes are drawn like in ScaleNote v1: with the sticky note tool you drag out the box and the note gets that size. A tap no longer makes a note; it tells you to drag. A note can be resized: choose the loop tool, tap the note, and drag the square on its bottom right corner. A note is never made smaller than its text needs. Notes already in your drawings are not changed.
- Shapes snap to a grid. Rectangles, ellipses, lines and arrows now put their corners on an invisible grid, so shapes drawn at different times line up exactly instead of missing each other by a pixel. It is on by default; turn it off in Settings, under Drawing, with "Snap shapes to a grid". Shapes already drawn are not moved.
- The pen colours are the same sixteen as in OneNote: three rows of six, six and four (yellow to light green, and black, two greys and light grey). Black is still the one that follows the theme. The colours are the same in light and dark, so dark grey is hard to see on the dark theme. Older drawings open as before; their red, blue, green, orange and purple are now the brighter versions.
- The bar that shows the line width has rounded ends.

## Fixed
- Zooming with two fingers on the drawing sheet no longer makes the drawing drift away. The spot between your fingers now stays under them while you zoom in or out, and moving both fingers together still moves the sheet.
- A page with an id but no text (for example one made by following a link to a page that does not exist yet) showed an empty "Kept exactly as written" block. It now opens as an empty page.

## Good to know
- A drawing that uses one of the new colours (yellow, pink, dark purple, raspberry, dark blue, light blue, light green, dark grey, grey, light grey) opens read-only in ScaleNote 0.4.3 and older, so use 0.4.4 on every device.
- A finger does not draw by default. Use a pen for the sticky note box, or turn on finger drawing.

## Known limits
- None of this has been tried yet on a real tablet with a pen and fingers.
- The other limits of 0.4.3 still apply.
