This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Share a page or folder with someone nearby.** Both of you choose a name in Settings, Sync, under Sharing with people. The other person also switches on **Let people nearby see my name to share with me** (it is off until they do). Then right-click (or long-press) a page or folder, choose **Share…** and pick their name. This works when your devices are on the same network: the same Wi-Fi, a phone's hotspot or a cable.
- **Nothing arrives unasked.** The other person sees "[Name] wants to share the folder Biology with you" and chooses Accept or No. When they accept, it appears in **Shared with me/<your name>** on all their devices. A shared page or folder has a small people mark in the list.
- **You can both change it, also offline.** While one of you has a shared page open, changes arrive within a few seconds; otherwise shares are synced every 30 seconds, or when your devices meet again. Pictures and PDFs that the shared pages show come along.
- **Edits made on two devices are joined word by word.** This also works between your own devices: a page changed in different places on two devices is now joined instead of being kept twice. Only when both changed the same words are both versions kept, as before.
- **Stop sharing and Leave** are in the Share box. Neither one removes anyone's copy; it is just no longer synced.

## Good to know
- Both devices need 0.6.4. Sharing works between devices on the same network for now, not over Tailscale.
- Only the person who shared something can add people or stop sharing it.
- A device you pair after accepting a share gets the shared pages through your other devices.
