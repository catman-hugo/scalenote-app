# ScaleNote 0.3.7

This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- Page history. Every time a page is saved, the text it had before is kept as an older version (at most one every 5 minutes per page). Open the clock button next to the page title, or "Show the history of this page" in the search box (Ctrl+K). Pick a version (a lone version opens at once) to see what changed since; the page properties at the top are shown as one folded note: each changed part shows the page now and that version, and you can tick "Put this part back" on the parts you want. "Put back the chosen parts" changes only those; "Put back this whole version" restores everything. Before anything is put back, the page as it was is kept in the history too, so a restore can be undone the same way. A restored page keeps its own `id:` property.
- How long versions are kept: all of the last 24 hours, then one a day for 30 days, then one a month for a year, at most 100 per page and 32 MB per page, 256 MB in all. Older ones are removed by themselves.
- Export a page. "Export this page as markdown" in the search box makes a new folder next to your notes folder, inside "<notes folder> (exports)". It holds the page (colours converted to what other programs show, as in the Obsidian copy) and the pictures it uses. Nothing is ever replaced: a second export gets a new folder. Not offered on Android.
- Print or save as PDF. "Print this page or save it as PDF" in the search box opens the system print window, where you can also choose to save a PDF. Only the title and the text are printed. Not offered on Android.
- Settings has "Delete all history" (click twice).

## Where the history is kept
Older versions are in a hidden folder called `.history` inside your notes folder (the same way the trash is kept), not next to it. They are kept under the page's `id:` property, so renaming or moving a page keeps its history. Obsidian ignores hidden folders. Be aware that the history also holds text you deleted from a page: if you share or sync the notes folder, the history goes with it. "Delete all history" removes it.

## Known limits
- The history compares whole lines. A changed word shows as a changed line.
- A page that is changed by another program is not copied until it is saved here again.
- There is no automatic export of the whole notes folder as one PDF, and export does not rewrite links between pages.
- Printing and PDF have not been tried on a real Windows or Linux computer yet.
