This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Page history.** Older versions of a page are kept automatically. The clock button shows what changed and puts back chosen parts or a whole version.
- **Export.** "Export this page as markdown" copies the page and its pictures to a new folder. Not on Android.
- **Print or save as PDF** from the search box. Not on Android.
- **Delete all history** in Settings.
