This is still an early test version: keep your own backup of anything you care about.

## New
- **Storage in Settings.** A new Storage tab shows how much room ScaleNote takes on this device: in all, per kind (text, ink, pictures, PDFs, other files) and per folder. Click a folder to look inside it. Pictures and PDFs count towards the folder of the page that shows them; the trash, page history and sync data have rows of their own.
- **Free up space.** On a device that keeps only part of your notes (Available offline), the Storage tab says how much is here but not kept, and **Free up space** removes it. Only files another of your own devices has in exactly this version are removed, so nothing is lost; the tab says how many files stay and why.
- **Search finds pages that are not on this device.** Pages that live only on your other devices are found by their title, with a cloud icon, and open from another device. The search says when the text of such pages was not searched.
- **Search their text on your other devices.** One click in the search box asks your paired devices in reach to search the text of the pages that are not here. What they find is shown with the device's name, and opens like any page.

## Fixed
- Free up space could count a copy on another person's device (from a share) as if it were on one of your own devices. Only your own devices count now.

## Good to know
- Searching on other devices needs those devices on 0.6.5, with sync on, in reach (same network or Tailscale). Only your own paired devices can be asked; people you share with cannot search your notes.
